STITCH BRIEF — BUILDER BASE V2 — EXPLORATION 01
Scope: NAVBAR + HERO + FIRST TRANSITION ONLY (about 1 to 1.5 screens). Do not design anything below the first transition.

WHO THIS IS FOR
Builder Base is the distribution layer for builders in India. It connects builders with the technology companies, products and ecosystems that want to reach them, through real rooms, build days and hackathons across Indian cities. It is not an event website, not a conference, not a Web3 community and not a student club.
Two audiences must both feel it in the first screen:
- Builders: "I need to be part of this."
- Technology companies: "If we want to reach builders in India, we talk to these people."

THE FEELING
Scale that is felt, not claimed. Momentum, credibility, culture, belonging, a little FOMO. Somewhere between a technology company, an editorial magazine and a cultural movement. Premium restraint first, immersive photography second, crowd energy third, a small dose of raw builder-internet texture.

FIXED CONTENT (use exactly)
- Wordmark: "builder base" in lowercase as a placeholder wordmark, or a simple two-stroke "b" monogram followed by the wordmark.
- Nav links: Events, Projects, Stories, About. Separate right-side link: "For partners ↗".
- Headline: "The distribution layer for builders." (may break as THE DISTRIBUTION / LAYER FOR BUILDERS.)
- Actions: "Join the base" (primary) and "Partner with us" (secondary).
- Metadata line (mono, small): "BUILDING ACROSS INDIA". Do not invent city names, coordinates, numbers or dates.
- No member counts, follower counts, statistics, logos of other companies, testimonials or event dates anywhere.
- Optional supporting line under the headline, max 12 words, plain and specific. Leave it out if the composition is stronger without it.

PHOTOGRAPHY RULES
- Do NOT generate people or AI scenes. Use clearly marked placeholder image blocks in neutral warm grey, each with a short caption describing the intended real photo, for example "[PHOTO: packed hackathon room seen from among the builders, warm tungsten light, laptops open]".
- Photos must be the dominant visual object, not small thumbnails.
- Legibility comes from a subtle bottom vignette on the photo, not a flat dark overlay across the whole image.

COLOUR (provisional, for this exploration only)
- Warm paper #F4F2ED, ink #121317, warm grey #5E5B55, near-black #0E0F13 for dark photographic areas.
- One brand colour, Builder Base indigo #2B2F7C, used ONLY for the primary action and at most one small identifier. On dark backgrounds use the tint #A7AEFF instead.
- No gradients except the photo vignette. No glass panels, no glows, no purple-blue AI gradients, no neon, no acid green.

TYPOGRAPHY
- One strong grotesk for headline and body (use Schibsted Grotesk; fallback Inter). One monospace for metadata (JetBrains Mono).
- Headline is enormous: it should occupy a large part of the viewport width, tight tracking (about -0.04em), line height about 0.92.
- Body text never below 16px. Mono metadata 12 to 13px uppercase.
- Uppercase only for the headline when intentional and for mono metadata. Everything else sentence case.

NAVBAR RULES
- Minimal: wordmark left, four links, "For partners ↗" right. Thin, quiet, no pill backgrounds, no shadow. A single "Join the base" button in the nav is allowed only if it is not repeated with equal weight in the hero.

DESIGN THREE SUBSTANTIALLY DIFFERENT COMPOSITIONS
Same content and rules, different structure. Do not make three variations of one layout.

COMPOSITION 1 — "The framed room" (light, photograph as an object)
- Paper background. The hero photo sits as a single large rounded panel (radius about 20px) inset from the viewport edges by about 24 to 40px, filling most of the first screen.
- The navbar sits on paper ABOVE the photo panel, not over it.
- The headline sits inside the photo, bottom-left, in white, over the bottom vignette. The two actions sit on the same baseline at bottom-right inside the photo.
- The mono metadata line sits in the top-left corner inside the photo.
- FIRST TRANSITION: below the panel, on paper, a horizontal row of 4 to 6 photo placeholders of different widths bleeding off the right edge, with the label "YOU HAD TO BE THERE." set large in ink above them and short mono captions under each photo (use "[CITY] / [ACTIVATION]" as literal placeholders).
- Mobile (375px): panel margin drops to 12px, photo becomes portrait 4:5, headline stacks over 3 to 4 lines inside the bottom of the photo, actions stack full-width below the panel on paper.

COMPOSITION 2 — "Type first, room below" (light, typography as the hero)
- Paper background. The headline is set in ink at the very largest size, spanning nearly the full width, starting just below the navbar. It is the first thing seen.
- A full-bleed wide photograph begins below the headline and runs to the bottom of the screen and beyond. The last line of the headline overlaps the top edge of the photo, so the type straddles paper and photo.
- The two actions and the mono metadata sit in a thin row between the headline and photo, left and right aligned, small and quiet.
- FIRST TRANSITION: the full-bleed photo continues; as it ends, it cuts hard to a paper section containing three short words in a row, "Discover. Build. Distribute.", each with one line of plain explanation, set with lots of space. No icons, no cards.
- Mobile (375px): headline fills the width over 4 to 5 lines, photo becomes full-width 4:5 below, actions stack.

COMPOSITION 3 — "Night room" (dark, cinematic full-bleed)
- The hero photograph fills the entire first screen edge to edge, with the navbar in white directly over the top of the photo.
- The headline is huge, white, placed low in the frame over a stronger bottom vignette, aligned to a 12-column grid with a deliberately offset second line.
- Along the very bottom edge, a thin mono slate row like a film frame: "BUILDING ACROSS INDIA" left, "[CITY] / [ACTIVATION]" placeholder centre, "SCROLL" right.
- The two actions sit directly under the headline, primary in indigo tint #A7AEFF with ink text, secondary as a plain white text link with ↗.
- FIRST TRANSITION: a hard cut from the dark photo to a bright paper section. On paper, the label "YOU HAD TO BE THERE." in very large ink type, and under it a strip of narrow portrait-orientation photo slices at equal height (one wider, the rest narrow), like an accordion, with mono captions.
- Mobile (375px): photo crops to portrait, headline 4 to 5 lines in the lower half, slate row reduces to "BUILDING ACROSS INDIA" only, accordion becomes a horizontally swipeable row.

FOR EACH COMPOSITION, DELIVER
- Desktop 1440px wide, showing the navbar, hero and the start of the first transition (about 1.5 screens tall).
- Mobile 375px of the same.

DO NOT
- Do not add a "next event", RSVP button, countdown, ticket, date block or event card to the hero.
- Do not add statistics, counters, logos, testimonials, bento grids, feature cards, glassmorphism, 3D shapes, blobs or decorative gradients.
- Do not use pixel fonts, terminal windows or crypto styling.
- Do not use stock or AI-generated people.
- Do not fill every empty space. Whitespace is intentional.
